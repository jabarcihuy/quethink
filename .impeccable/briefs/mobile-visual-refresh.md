## Direction contract

Phone app for students learning databases and teachers managing real classes. Light, warm surfaces for daytime classroom use; strong indigo action panels, clear hierarchy, white grouped lists, explicit state, meaningful SVG iconography and varied density. Use existing color/type/shape tokens. Headline32/40 becomes visible on main screens; body16/24 remains readable. No decorative gradients, fictional stats, stock learning imagery, or repeated identical icon cards as a whole page. Native navigation and backend gates remain intact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: student home names the learner and real class, shows the next legitimate learning action, and reaches parallel Learn/Attendance destinations; teacher home highlights classes and real management navigation. Attendance presents a distinct QR action panel above compact session history. Materials display their real ordered progression with current/completed/locked status. No eyebrow labels above headings.

Signature interaction: press feedback on destination rows/tiles; all navigation remains real. No entrance motion or expensive decorative effects. Material48dp targets, safe areas, keyboard support, large-font single-column adaptation. No new global font or color primitive required. Native light scheme remains the current product setting; OS night-mode is checked for stable forced-light rendering, not claimed as a new dark-theme feature.
